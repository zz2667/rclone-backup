restic-backup-limpida

This is a private, single-user application. It lets the owner's own computer back up the owner's files to the owner's own Google Drive using rclone and restic.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect, sell or share any data.

这是一个仅供本人使用的私人应用：由本人的电脑通过 rclone 与 restic，把本人的文件备份到本人自己的 Google 云端硬盘。不对外提供服务，没有其他用户，不收集、出售或共享任何数据。
