Privacy Policy

restic-backup-limpida is a private application used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: files in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API scope requested at sign-in.
  • Purpose: storing and restoring the owner's encrypted backup data. Nothing else.
  • Storage: backup data stays in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Other users: the application has no users other than the owner.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

隐私政策

本应用仅供所有者本人使用：只访问本人 Google 云端硬盘中的文件，用于存放和恢复本人的加密备份数据；备份数据留在本人的 Google 云端硬盘，授权令牌只保存在本人电脑上；不向任何第三方共享、出售或转移数据；没有其他用户；可随时在 Google 账号权限页面撤销授权。
